# Mountain Mist Asset

`src/assets/mountain-mist.webp` is a transparent texture made with the built-in image generation tool, then converted to WebP (1440 x 720, about 180 KB). The existing mountain photograph is unchanged. The upper texture travels right and a different lower texture travels left. Each lane has four tiles; only their short tails crossfade with complementary masks. One-period translation at constant lane opacity removes hard texture cuts and clearing intervals without stacking entire banks. Soft vertical masks keep visible upper/lower lanes separate. Each tile keeps the earlier 160%-width, 65%-height scale, with the upper lane at 4% and the lower at -12% bottom. Motion pauses offscreen and respects reduced motion. No video or 3D renderer is loaded. Browser tests inspect actual composited alpha at 12 phases, including immediately before/after wrap, on mobile, landscape and desktop.

`src/assets/mountain-mist-low.webp` (2172 x 724, 315 KB) is a second generated alpha texture, converted to WebP without changing its alpha. Prompt: "One wide 3:1 transparent ribbon of soft fine morning mountain mist; irregular, thin translucent smoky veils, varied density and transparent gaps, curling horizontal threads; grayscale white/light gray, feathered edges, no repeating shapes, landscape, background, text or painted checkerboard." Generated using the built-in image tool.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent atmospheric texture for a website animation. Generate ONLY a broad horizontal wisp of delicate white mountain mist on an actually transparent alpha background. Wide landscape format. Soft, thin, semi-transparent irregular fog, fine organic billowing structure, thin strands trailing horizontally, very airy gaps between wisps; denser near the lower third with edges fading fully to transparent on all four sides. This will drift slowly over an existing photograph of a mountain. No mountain, landscape, sky, sun, scenery, text, hard edges, framed rectangle, checkerboard, dark backdrop, or separate round cartoon clouds. Keep the mist white/light neutral grey with realistic translucent vapor. Only the mist itself and transparent empty space.
